# SAP Transformation, Cybersecurity & Technology Risk

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focused on the rollout of a global SAP template at a manufacturing site in Istanbul as part of a large transformation program with a double-digit million euro investment volume.

I worked with SAP consultants, cybersecurity specialists, business process owners, local IT, program management and external implementation partners to identify and manage technology and cybersecurity risks throughout the rollout.

My role combined SAP project experience with cybersecurity and technology risk expertise. I was actively involved in discussions around access security, segregation of duties, software development lifecycle controls, testing, deployment, third-party dependencies and business continuity.

---

## 🎯 Business Challenge

The manufacturing site was moving onto a new SAP template while production and business operations had to continue without major disruption.

The rollout introduced significant technology and cybersecurity risks because critical business processes, user access, technical changes and external implementation partners all depended on the new SAP environment.

The main challenge was to make sure that the solution could go live securely and in a controlled way without creating unacceptable operational, access or implementation risks.

Key topics included:

- SAP access security and segregation of duties
- Software development lifecycle and change control
- Testing and release readiness
- Cutover and deployment risk
- Third-party and contract dependencies
- Business continuity during transition
- Overall technology and cyber risk before go-live

---

## 🧩 My Role

I was the cybersecurity and technology risk expert within the project and worked closely with SAP, business and technical teams.

My role was not limited to coordination. I reviewed the design and rollout approach from a risk and security perspective, challenged assumptions and worked with subject matter experts to understand where technical weaknesses could affect the go-live.

I regularly discussed issues with SAP functional consultants, SAP security specialists, developers, infrastructure teams and business process owners and helped translate identified risks into concrete remediation actions.

## 🔐 SAP Access Security

Access security was one of the most important areas because the new SAP template introduced new roles, permissions and responsibilities.

Together with SAP security and business teams, I reviewed topics such as:

- Role design
- Privileged access
- Segregation of duties
- Temporary project access
- User provisioning and approval

The focus was on making sure that users had the access required for their role without creating excessive privileges or conflicting responsibilities.

---

## 🧩 Cybersecurity & Technology Risk

The project was assessed from an end-to-end risk perspective rather than looking at individual controls in isolation.

Typical risk areas included:

- Unauthorized or excessive system access
- Inappropriate privileged access
- Conflicting SAP roles
- Insufficient testing of critical changes
- Uncontrolled transports into production
- Failed or incomplete deployment
- Dependency on external implementation partners

I worked with the relevant SMEs to understand the likelihood and potential business impact of these scenarios and to prioritize remediation accordingly.

---

## 🔄 SDLC, Change & Transport Management

The SAP solution moved through multiple development, test and production environments.

I reviewed whether changes were sufficiently controlled throughout the lifecycle.

Key topics included:

- Development and testing environments
- Change approval
- Transport management
- Emergency changes
- Defect remediation
- Production deployment

The objective was to reduce the risk that incomplete, unauthorized or insufficiently tested changes reached the production environment.

---

## 🧪 Testing & Release Readiness

Testing was a critical part of the risk assessment because the SAP template supported important operational processes.

Together with the project teams, I reviewed areas such as:

- Integration testing
- User acceptance testing
- Security-related test scenarios
- Defect prioritization and closure
- Go-live acceptance criteria

The focus was on understanding whether remaining defects or incomplete test coverage could create unacceptable business or cybersecurity risk.

---

## 🤝 Third-Party & Contract Risk

The rollout relied heavily on external SAP implementation partners and specialist providers.

I assessed risks related to:

- Delivery responsibilities
- Access by external consultants
- Contractual obligations
- Dependency on key resources
- Escalation and issue ownership

This was particularly important where project success or security depended on third-party delivery.
